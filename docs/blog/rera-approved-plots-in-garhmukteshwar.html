2. What Does RERA Approval Actually Mean for Plot Buyers?

The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act was enacted to protect home and plot buyers from fraudulent practices, delays, and hidden financial discrepancies. When a plotted township like Ganga Residency holds verified RERA compliance, it guarantees several statutory safeguards:

Title Clarity

Mandatory disclosure of land ownership titles, encumbrance certificates, and government land conversion approvals.

70% of buyer funds are strictly locked in designated escrow accounts to be used exclusively for land development.

Timely Delivery

Legal accountability binding developers to adhere strictly to promised project completion and possession timelines.
