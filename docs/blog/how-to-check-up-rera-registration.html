2. Step-by-Step Procedure to Check UP RERA Registration Online

The Uttar Pradesh Real Estate Regulatory Authority provides a fully digitized public portal (up-rera.in) where anyone can inspect registered projects, agents, promoters, and quarterly progress reports. Follow these four essential steps to conduct your search:

01

Visit Official Portal

Navigate to the official UP RERA website and locate the 'Projects' tab on the main landing menu.

02

Select Project Type

Choose 'Registered Projects' and enter the specific promoter name, enterprise, or project title.

03

Enter District

Filter your search by selecting 'Hapur' or 'Garhmukteshwar' from the Uttar Pradesh district dropdown menu.

04

Verify Certificate

Download and review the official RERA registration certificate, layout approvals, and validity dates.

3. Key Parameters to Verify in a RERA Certificate

Once you pull up a project's RERA registration details on the portal, do not just check the registration number. You must thoroughly audit specific statutory disclosures to ensure complete safety:

RERA Parameter What to Look For Why It Matters for Buyers
Project Validity & Completion Date Check proposed start and end dates declared by the developer. Ensures legal accountability for timely delivery without unapproved delays.
Land Title & Ownership Verify whether the promoter owns the land or holds a valid joint development agreement. Protects against fraudulent third-party claims and litigated property titles.
Litigation History Review active complaints, legal notices, or penalties recorded against the promoter. Highlights developer credibility and previous dispute resolutions.
Quarterly Progress Reports (QPR) Inspect uploaded site photographs and financial expenditure milestones. Validates actual physical development on the ground against promises.

4. Essential Document Checklist Beyond RERA Verification

While UP RERA registration is the gold standard of compliance, prudent investors should cross-verify additional local statutory clearances. Review this expert 4-point verification checklist before paying your booking token:

1
Section 143 Land Use Conversion

Ensure the agricultural land has been formally converted to residential use under Uttar Pradesh land revenue codes.

2
Local Development Authority Approvals

Check layout sanction plans approved by local planning authorities or regional development boards in UP.

3
Clear Title Chain Documents

Review 30-year encumbrance certificates (EC) and legal title opinions issued by independent advocates.

4
Bank Loan Approvals

Verify if leading nationalized and private banks have approved project financing, as banks conduct rigorous legal due diligence.
